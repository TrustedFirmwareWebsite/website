## Announcing New Mbed TLS Maintainers and Future Stewardship

__[Cambridgeshire, UK – November 25, 2026]__ Trusted Firmware is pleased to announce that Linaro will take on maintenance and stewardship of Mbed TLS, with the support of NXP and Espressif, ensuring the continued development, maintenance and security response capabilities of the widely used TLS and x.509 library beyond April 2027.

The transition will be supported by the community and dedicated engineering contributions from NXP and Espressif, bringing together industry expertise and resources to support the project’s long-term sustainability. Mbed TLS will remain under [TrustedFirmware.org](https://www.trustedfirmware.org/) governance, preserving its open development model and community participation.

### Project Transition Background

Mbed TLS is an open-source cryptographic and TLS/X.509 library that secures a very large number of embedded and connected devices across the IoT ecosystem.

The existing Mbed TLS maintainers are ramping down their efforts on the TLS and X.509 libraries, and from April 2027 they will cease development and maintenance of those libraries. Without new maintainers ramping up by then, security incident response, security fixes, and releases for those libraries will cease, while the existing maintainers continue developing and maintaining TF-PSA-Crypto.

Trusted Firmware and the broader community express their deepest gratitude to the outgoing maintainers and stewards for their unwavering dedication, leadership, and invaluable contributions to Mbed TLS over the years. With their efforts we have established a strong foundation for the library's ongoing security and success.

### A Collaborative Transition to Secure the Future of Mbed TLS

In response to the transition, several organizations expressed interest in supporting the project. Linaro has agreed to take on the lead maintenance and stewardship role, working alongside incoming engineers from NXP and Espressif and collaborating with the current Mbed TLS team to ensure a smooth transition.

This commitment builds on Linaro’s existing experience with Mbed TLS and its broader work across the open source embedded software ecosystem, where it has long supported collaborative engineering efforts spanning various industry stakeholders.

"It is really encouraging to see both members and non-members stepping up across the community to support Mbed TLS during this transition, that collaborative effort is what keeps open source projects healthy and moving forward." __Shaun Longhorn, Community Manager, Trusted Firmware__

### A Call for Continued Support

Mbed TLS’s new stewardship will provide the engineering leadership and resources required to sustain maintenance, while the project remains open to contributions from organizations and individuals across the ecosystem.

Expanding the pool of maintainers is essential for the long-term sustainability of the project. This success relies directly on active community leadership alongside sustainable co-funding and dedicated engineering contributions from ecosystem stakeholders. Flexible co-funding models through Linaro allow Trusted Firmware members, key contributors and other interested parties to share resource commitments or engage in 1:1 support.

To get involved, [connect with the community](https://www.trustedfirmware.org/faq/) on the `#mbed-tls-dev` Discord channel, join the Mbed TLS mailing list, and check out the [Mbed TLS project meeting page](https://www.trustedfirmware.org/meetings/) on [TrustedFirmware.org](https://www.trustedfirmware.org/).
